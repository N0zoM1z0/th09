## RunEcl full-owner baseline

The current source-cold pinned-VC7.1 `EclManager.obj` reproduces the reviewed
15,564-byte physical owner and all 598 bound relocation fields. The original
switch/table data is aligned and agrees. Exactly 30 ordinary bytes differ,
all in two opcode bodies:

- Opcode 4 (`JUMP`), offsets `0x278, 0x27A..0x27E, 0x280`: 7 bytes.
  The original reads the raw signed timer operand into ECX, pushes it, then
  materializes the active-context timer receiver and calls the genuine
  `ZunTimer::operator=(int)` at `0x00401500`.
- Opcode 155 (`SET_TIMEOUT_SPELL`), offsets `0x30B0..0x30B7`,
  `0x30B9..0x30C2`, `0x30C4..0x30C6`, `0x30C8`, and `0x30CA`: 23 bytes.
  The original updates only primary-flag bit 24 using its observed masked-XOR
  sequence, then advances and redispatches.

The independent opcode-4 behavioral audit exercised 90 combinations of timer
values, instruction offsets and contexts; target/candidate timer assignment
and advancement agreed. The opcode-155 audit decoded the original and candidate
x86 instruction prefixes and checked 3,072 combinations (12 representative
32-bit flag words × all 256 wire-byte values); the bit transition agreed.
Both are semantic diagnostics only, not byte-exactness credit.

Rejected source experiments were restored:

- Replacing the opcode-4 local scalar copy with a direct raw-operand assignment
  increased the full-owner ordinary residual from 30 to 42 bytes.
- Reordering opcode-155 local reads to encourage target register allocation
  caused a much larger owner/relocation/table mismatch and was rejected.

## EnemyManager::OnUpdate full-owner baseline

The current source-cold pinned-VC7.1 object preserves the complete 3,900-byte
physical owner, all 97 relocation fields, 1,026 decoded instructions, 69
ordered direct calls, 170 matching direct blocks, and the four switch-table
destinations. Thirteen ordinary bytes differ at:

`0x19C, 0x1A3, 0x252, 0x263, 0x26D, 0x272, 0x294, 0x29A, 0x29C, 0x2A5, 0x2A9, 0x2B7, 0x2BD`.

The first two are draw-group index register scheduling. The remaining eleven
are register scheduling around the special-attack side/EffectManager access,
threshold comparison, and 0x214-byte bullet-spawn descriptor construction and
position copy. All 97 relocation fields agree; no field or switch-table drift
was observed. Source-shape trials using direct side-state access, a temporary
Float3 copy, a descriptor pointer, and sequential draw-head pointer arithmetic
were byte-neutral and were discarded.

`python3 scripts/check-boss-frontiers.py --owner runecl --skip-exact-siblings`
and `--owner enemy --skip-exact-siblings` both completed successfully. These
are the two requested boss frontiers, not the all-owner cold replay. The RunEcl

bytes, zero field/table differences, eight canonical angle callsites, and

produced 42 ordinary differences. None of these variants is retained.
